# TravelHub Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}} · [อ่านภาษาไทย](../th/)

This policy explains how TravelHub ("we") collects, uses, and discloses personal data when you use the TravelHub mobile app, in line with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) ("PDPA").

## 1. Who we are
The data controller is **{{CONTROLLER_NAME}}**. Contact for any privacy matter: **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**.

## 2. Data we collect
| Data | Source | Why | Legal basis (PDPA) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account: name, email, sign-in method (Apple, Google, or email), profile photo from your Google account if any | You / Apple / Google | Create and secure your account | Contract (s.24(3)) |
| Trip data: trip names, destinations, dates, accommodation, stops, notes you type (for example names of people you meet), and the time you tap **Arrived** at each stop | You | Plan and run your trips | Contract (s.24(3)) |
| Precise location, only while the app is open and only if you allow it | Your device | Show you on today's route and calculate travel time to your next stop | Contract (s.24(3)); you can refuse or turn it off at any time and the trip still works |
| Place searches and stop coordinates | You | Find places and calculate routes | Contract (s.24(3)) |
| Crash and error reports (device model, OS version, app version, error details; no location or email) | Your device | Fix bugs and keep the app stable | Legitimate interest (s.24(5)) |

We do **not** track your location in the background, we do **not** save the path you travel, and we do **not** share your location with other users. We do not sell personal data, use advertising networks, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 3. Who receives your data
We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:
- **Supabase** (database, sign-in), servers in Singapore.
- **Google Maps Platform** (Places, Routes, Maps SDK). Your searches and the coordinates needed to calculate a route, including your current location while you navigate, are sent through our server to Google. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Sentry** (crash reports).
- **Apple / Google** when you choose to sign in with them.

Booking or partner links open the partner's website; we do not send your personal data to the partner.

## 4. Transfers outside Thailand
Our providers store or process data outside Thailand (Singapore, the United States, and other countries). We rely on providers with appropriate data protection standards and contractual safeguards, as required by PDPA sections 28–29.

## 5. How long we keep data
We keep account and trip data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and trip data within 30 days; backup copies are overwritten within 7 days. Crash reports are kept for up to 30 days.

## 6. Your rights
Under the PDPA you may: access and get a copy of your data; ask us to correct it; ask us to delete it; object to or restrict processing; ask to transfer your data; and withdraw consent where we rely on consent. Contact **{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}**. You can also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee ([pdpc.or.th](https://www.pdpc.or.th)).

You can delete your account in the app (**Profile › Delete account**) or on the web at [Delete your account](../../delete-account/).

## 7. Children
TravelHub is intended for users aged 13 and over.

## 8. Security
Data is encrypted in transit, access to our database is restricted per user, and server-side API keys are never stored in the app.

## 9. Changes
We will post any update on this page and change the effective date; for significant changes we will tell you in the app.
